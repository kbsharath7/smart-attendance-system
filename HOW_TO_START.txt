HOW TO OPEN THE APP (no commands needed)
=========================================

EVERY DAY
  Double-click  Start_Attendance_App.bat
  - A black window opens. KEEP IT OPEN.
  - Your browser opens the app by itself in a few seconds.
  - Log in. (Admin: admin / admin123)
  - To stop the app, close the black window.

ONLY THE FIRST TIME ON A NEW COMPUTER
  Double-click  Setup_First_Time.bat
  - Keep the internet ON. It takes 10 to 25 minutes.
  - It installs everything and puts an "Attendance App" icon on the Desktop.
  - Do NOT run it on a computer where the app already works.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then "Run anyway".
Camera features (Take attendance, Class monitoring) open a camera window on the same computer.
